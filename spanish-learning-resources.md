# Spanish learning resources: the best free ones in 2026

These are the free Spanish learning resources we would actually use, grouped by what they do: apps, YouTube, podcasts, reading, grammar, dictionaries, conversation, and flashcards. Pick one from each group, not ten. At the end there is a free study week that uses only this list.
{: .lede }

Every resource here is free, or has a free tier that is useful on its own. We link only to home pages, because deep links break. "Level" is a rough guide: beginner (first months), intermediate (you follow slow conversations), advanced (you follow native-speed content with some effort).

🎯 **Key point:** Most of your time should go to listening and reading that you mostly understand. Grammar sites and dictionaries are tools you open when a question comes up, not the main course.
{: .key }

## YouTube channels

We link to a YouTube search for each channel, so you always land on the right one. Watch in Spanish with Spanish subtitles if you need help, and turn them off once you follow most of a video. Lessons in English are good for questions, but count only videos in Spanish as listening time.

| Name | What it is | Level | Cost | Why it is good |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Dreaming+Spanish) | Comprehensible input videos with drawings and gestures | Absolute beginner to advanced | Free | You understand a lot without translation |
| [Easy Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Easy+Spanish) | Street interviews with Spanish and English subtitles, filmed in several countries | Intermediate | Free | Real people at real speed, with help on screen |
| [Butterfly Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Butterfly+Spanish) | Lessons in English about grammar, phrases, and pronunciation | Beginner | Free | Clear answers to the questions beginners ask |

## Podcasts

Search for these in your podcast app. Podcasts are the easiest way to add listening time, because you can do it while you walk, cook, or drive. If an episode is too hard, play it at 0.8x speed or go back to an easier show for a few weeks.

| Name | What it is | Level | Cost | Why it is good |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Coffee Break Spanish](https://coffeebreaklanguages.com/) | Lessons where a teacher and a learner work through Spanish together | Beginner to intermediate | Free episodes; paid extras | Clear structure, and you learn with someone at your level |
| Duolingo Spanish Podcast | True stories told in easy Spanish, with short English narration | Intermediate | Free | Good stories that keep you listening |
| Españolistos | Two hosts talk about everyday topics in clear Latin American Spanish | Intermediate | Free | Natural conversation, not a script |
| Hoy Hablamos | Short episodes in Spanish from Spain on culture and daily life | Intermediate | Free; paid transcripts | Easy to fit into every day |
| [Radio Ambulante](https://radioambulante.org/) | Long-form stories from across Latin America, distributed by NPR | Advanced | Free | Great storytelling and many accents |

## Reading

Reading lets you go at your own speed, so it is a good partner for listening. Choose texts where you know most of the words on a page. If you need the dictionary every line, the text is too hard for now.

| Name | What it is | Level | Cost | Why it is good |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Spanish stories for beginners](/spanish-stories-for-beginners/) | Our short stories written for learners | Beginner | Free | Short, simple, and made to read in one sitting |
| Graded readers (for example Olly Richards' *Short Stories in Spanish for Beginners*) | Books written with limited words for learners | Beginner to intermediate | Free at many libraries; paid to buy | Whole stories you can finish, which builds reading speed |
| [Vikidia](https://es.vikidia.org/) | A Spanish encyclopedia written for children | Lower intermediate | Free | Real topics in simple sentences |
| [BBC News Mundo](https://www.bbc.com/mundo) | News in Spanish | Intermediate to advanced | Free | Clear, neutral writing about topics you already know |
| [Wikipedia en español](https://es.wikipedia.org/) | The Spanish Wikipedia | Advanced | Free | Read about your hobbies in Spanish |
| [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/) | Free public domain books, including Spanish classics | Advanced | Free | Classic literature for when you are ready |

💡 **Tip:** Read news about a story you already know in English. Your background knowledge fills the gaps, so you can read harder Spanish than you think.

## Dictionaries

A good dictionary shows examples, not only one translation. When you look up a word, read two or three example sentences and listen to the audio. Spanish words can change meaning by country, so check the regional notes when a word looks strange.

| Name | What it is | Level | Cost | Why it is good |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [SpanishDict](https://www.spanishdict.com/) | Spanish-English dictionary with examples and audio | All levels | Free (ads) | Clear, learner-friendly entries |
| [WordReference](https://www.wordreference.com/) | Dictionary plus a large forum of language questions | All levels | Free | The forums answer questions no dictionary does |
| [Linguee](https://www.linguee.com/) | Search engine for real translated sentences | Intermediate | Free | Shows how a word is really used in context |
| [Reverso Context](https://context.reverso.net/) | Translations of phrases with many examples | Intermediate | Free; paid extras | Great for phrases and expressions |
| [Forvo](https://forvo.com/) | Pronunciation of words by native speakers | All levels | Free | Hear the same word from Spain, Mexico, Argentina, and more |

## Conversation exchange

You help someone with English, and they help you with Spanish. Free, and much less scary than it sounds. Agree on the split before you start (for example, 15 minutes in each language), and prepare three topics so the talk does not stop.

| Name | What it is | Level | Cost | Why it is good |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Tandem](https://www.tandem.net/) | App to find exchange partners for text, voice, and video | Beginner and up | Free; paid extras | Many Spanish speakers who want to learn English |
| [HelloTalk](https://www.hellotalk.com/) | Chat app for exchanges, with built-in corrections | Beginner and up | Free; paid extras | Text first, so you can start before you feel ready to talk |
| [ConversationExchange.com](https://www.conversationexchange.com/) | Website to find partners online or in your city | Intermediate | Free | Simple, and good for meeting in person |
| [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/) | Local events, including language exchange nights | Intermediate | Mostly free | Real conversations with real people near you |

## Flashcards

Flashcards keep the words you meet in your listening and reading. Make cards from words you heard or read this week, with an example sentence, and keep new cards to about 10 a day.

| Name | What it is | Level | Cost | Why it is good |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Anki](https://ankiweb.net/) | Flashcard app with spaced repetition | All levels | Free (iOS app is paid) | The most powerful free review tool |
| [Quizlet](https://quizlet.com/) | Simple flashcard sets and games | Beginner | Free tier with ads | Easy to start, many sets made by teachers |
| [Clozemaster](https://www.clozemaster.com/) | Fill in the missing word in real sentences | Intermediate | Free tier | Learn words inside sentences, not alone |

## A free study week using only these resources

This plan takes about 40 minutes a day. If you only have 20 minutes, do the listening and the words and skip the rest.

| Day | Listening (20 min) | Words (10 min) | Speaking or writing (10 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Dreaming Spanish video at your level | Make 10 Anki cards from words you heard | Language Transfer: one lesson, answer out loud |
| Tuesday | Coffee Break Spanish episode | Anki review, then 5 new cards | Write 5 sentences about your day, check them with SpanishDict |
| Wednesday | Dreaming Spanish or Easy Spanish video | Anki review | Language Transfer: one lesson |
| Thursday | One graded reader chapter, read and say it out loud | Anki review, then 5 new cards | Send 3 voice messages to a HelloTalk or Tandem partner |
| Friday | Easy Spanish video, then the same video again without subtitles | Anki review | Watch one Butterfly Spanish or Señor Jordan video on a grammar question you had this week |
| Saturday | A podcast episode on a walk | Anki review | 15-minute call with an exchange partner (Spanish half the time) |
| Sunday | Something fun: a video on your hobby, in Spanish | Anki review only | Write what you learned this week in 5 lines, in Spanish |

⚠️ **Watch out:** Free resources make it easy to collect and hard to commit. Use the same 3 or 4 resources for a full month before you switch.
{: .warn }

Repeat this week four times, and move up one level in your videos and podcasts when you understand most of what you hear.
